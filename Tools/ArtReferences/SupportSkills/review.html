Offline support skills

Verified Unity scene captures · 7 September 2026. Behavior and source notes

Eight skills now cast with original actions and artwork. Magic Guard and Magic Armor are available through earned SP; the other presets provide practice access while their progression remains separate.

Magic Guard

Learned with earned SP and cast through an assigned quickslot. The original NX use effect accompanies the buff icon.

Magic Armor: character echo

The source effect redraws the equipped character, grows it and fades it over 500 milliseconds.

Magic Armor in the scene

The buff increases weapon defense. Its casting effect ends independently of the longer stat buff.

Hyper Body

Maximum HP/MP rise while current resources retain their values after paying the cast cost. The two contributions share one buff icon.

Practice selector at 640 × 480

Six explicit job presets teach level-one buffs. They preserve earned points, items and current resources.

Iron Body

Uses the same source character echo. Its normal Endure prerequisite chain remains unported.

Rage

The NX attack bonus and defense penalty are applied together.

Iron Will

The original cast effect accompanies both defense bonuses.

Fire/Poison Meditation

Applies the NX magic-attack bonus; the existing source magic damage formula is unchanged.

Ice/Lightning Meditation

The second Meditation skill uses its own NX icon and effect.
