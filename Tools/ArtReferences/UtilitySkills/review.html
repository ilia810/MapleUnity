Focus with earned first-job SP

Amazon Blessing 3 unlocks Focus in the ordinary skill book. Casting uses the original effect and active buff icon; accuracy and avoidability affect combat.

Dark Sight

All assembled character layers become translucent. Labels, HUD and the native casting burst remain legible. Concealment blocks new attacks and contact damage.

Cancel through the buff icon

Right-clicking the icon restores the character and removes that source's remaining stat contributions. A newer buff replacing one of its stats survives cancellation.

Contact evasion

Nimble Body contributes real avoidability. This deterministic scene forces an evaded contact to verify native MISS artwork, unchanged HP/MP and the contact grace period.
